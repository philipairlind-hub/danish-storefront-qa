## ICP (hvem køber)

- **Primær:** Udenlandske DTC-brands og webshops (SE, NO, FI, DE, NL, UK, US) med 1–50 ansatte, som har lanceret eller er ved at lancere en dansk version af deres butik, og som har oversat den med AI eller maskinoversættelse. Mønsteret er dokumenteret: Et svensk dyrebrand oversatte sit site fra svensk med AI og hyrede bagefter en indfødt korrekturlæser på Upwork.
- **Sekundær:** Lokaliseringsbureauer (LSP'er) og AI-datafirmaer, der skal have en dansk slutkontrol.
- **Ikke:** Danske virksomheder (projektregel for outbound), enterprise-retailere med egne lokaliseringsteams, og opgaver der kræver juridisk rådgivning eller telefonmøder.

## Positionering (én sætning)

> A native Danish speaker checks your AI-translated Danish store the way a Danish customer reads it, starting from a machine scan of the live pages, and hands you a fix list you can paste in.

Det smalle løfte: **fast pris, asynkront, på den publicerede side, med en fejllog.** Vi lover ikke flere salg, bedre SEO eller juridisk compliance.

## Landing page / Project Catalog-tekst (engelsk, klar til ejergodkendelse)

**Headline:** Your Danish store was translated by AI. Has a Dane read it?

**Subhead:** Native Danish review of your live storefront, plus a machine scan for leftover Swedish, English and formatting errors. Fixed price, written delivery, no calls needed.

**What you get**
1. **Machine pre-scan of your live Danish pages.** It flags leftover English or Swedish UI text ("Sign up", "Sök"), untranslated theme keys, non-DKK prices, decimal points in prices and missing `lang`/`hreflang`.
2. **Native read-through.** Wording that is grammatical but unnatural, wrong compound words (særskrivning), inconsistent terms, tone (du/De), and dates and addresses in Danish format.
3. **An issue log.** Each row gives the page, the original text, the problem, a suggested fix and a severity. You also get the corrected text, ready to paste.

**FAQ / objections**
- *"We can just ask ChatGPT to check it."* — You can. It will catch some errors and miss whether the text sounds like a Danish shop. That part needs a native reader who answers for the result.
- *"Isn't this what MakesYouLocal does?"* — MakesYouLocal offers translation (also file-upload AI translation with native review), audits and full market entry. This is narrower: a fixed-price, written check of what is already live, done by one native Danish reviewer.
- *"Will you translate it for us?"* — No. This is review and correction of existing Danish text. Full translation from scratch is out of scope.
- *"Do you guarantee more sales?"* — No. You get fewer errors and a store that reads as Danish. Conversion depends on much more.
- *"Will you buy something to test checkout?"* — No. The flow is tested in your test mode or with test data you provide. No real payments are made, and no personal data is used.

**When NOT to buy**
- Your Danish site is written by Danish staff or a professional Danish agency.
- You need legal review of terms, GPSR or consumer law. That calls for a lawyer.
- You need Swedish, Norwegian or Finnish review. Only Danish is offered today.
- You need it in under 24 hours.
