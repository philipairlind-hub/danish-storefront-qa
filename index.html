What the scan flags

  • Shopify and app keys shown as Translation missing: da…
  • English buttons and labels: Add to cart, Sign up, Search
  • Swedish leftovers: Sök, Köpvillkor, a Swedish slogan
  • Prices in EUR or SEK, and 199.95 kr instead of 199,95 kr.
  • A page that tells browsers and Google it is not Danish

What it can't judge

  • Whether the Danish reads naturally or sounds machine-made
  • Compound-word errors (særskrivning) and du/De tone
  • Your checkout flow, emails and anything behind a login
  • Text that your shop builds with JavaScript after loading

That part is the native review below.

Questions

We can ask ChatGPT to check it. Why pay?

You can, and it will catch some errors. It will miss whether the text sounds like a Danish shop. That needs a native reader who answers for the result.

Do you translate from scratch?

No. This is review and correction of Danish text that already exists. Full translation is out of scope.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. You get fewer errors and a store that reads as Danish. Conversion depends on much more.

Will you place a real order to test checkout?

No. The flow is tested in your test mode or with test data you provide. No real payments, no personal data.

When is this not for you?

If your Danish site is written by Danish staff or a professional Danish agency, if you need legal review of terms or product-safety rules, if you need Swedish, Norwegian or Finnish review, or if you need it within 24 hours.
