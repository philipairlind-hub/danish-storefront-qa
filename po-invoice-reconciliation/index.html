Inputs and what gets flagged

Your three existing CSV exports

  • Purchase order lines with PO, SKU, quantity and unit cost
  • Receipt lines with PO/SKU, quantities and document IDs
  • Invoice lines with PO/SKU, quantities, unit price and invoice IDs

Exact column mapping must be agreed before acceptance. Request a quote using column names only; do not email customer or supplier records until a safe transfer method is agreed.

Human-review flags

  • Partial or missing receipts
  • Invoice quantity greater than received/ordered quantity
  • Invoice price differences and missing invoice
  • Unknown PO/SKU keys or duplicate document lines
  • Conflicting currencies or units of measure

What this is not

Not accounting advice, 3-way payment approval, a Shopify purchase order import, invoice OCR, an ERP connector or a replacement for your accountant. No automatic invoice settlement, inventory write or external data upload. Manual review remains essential.
