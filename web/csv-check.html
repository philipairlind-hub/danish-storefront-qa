Review summary

    Limitations: The checker examines CSV record widths, headers, repeated SKU candidates, and basic prices where recognizable columns exist. It does not map arbitrary supplier fields, validate your complete Shopify schema, check all images/metafields, verify SKU uniqueness in your store, inspect stock levels, or perform any import. Repeated SKUs or missing handles can be intentional. Always review the output yourself.
