Questions before you buy

Does the workbench send follow-ups or recover sales automatically?

No. It does not send emails, texts or CRM updates, and it cannot promise additional revenue. A human decides whether an estimate is valid, contact is permissible, and follow-up is appropriate.

Do you receive or store our customer CSV?

This page accepts no file upload. The downloaded HTML tool runs on your computer and does not require a network service. Do not email personal customer exports before agreeing on handling and scope.

What file format and browser do I need?

Use a UTF-8 CSV, up to 10 MB and 20,000 records, with dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. Open the delivered workbench in a current desktop Chrome or Edge browser.

What does the one-time license cover?

The delivered version may be used internally by one purchasing business. It is not a subscription and does not include a custom integration or ongoing managed service.

Can I commission a manual export review instead?

A separate $149 one-time manual audit may be available subject to written agreement on row count, scope and delivery. Contact shopairlind@gmail.com before sharing files or paying.
