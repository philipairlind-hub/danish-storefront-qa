What you can preserve and what cannot be reconstructed

  1. Save Stocky's available reports for historical purchase orders, stocktakes, stock-levels/cost data and relevant transfer activity while access remains.
  2. Retain originals unchanged and preserve copies with clear dates, filenames and checksums.
  3. Check for missing columns, malformed CSV rows, duplicates, blank fields and ambiguous identifiers before anyone treats the archive as complete.
  4. Document unavoidable gaps: supplier contacts are not exportable, and historical PO statuses/receipts cannot simply be imported into new Shopify purchase orders.
  5. Continue operations using Shopify's built-in inventory tools or a purpose-built external inventory application, rather than treating a CSV archive as live stock.

Excluded: extracting data from a disabled Stocky account, downloading your supplier contacts, recovering deleted history, migrating historical PO states, editing Shopify inventory, reconstructing unexported records, tax/accounting certification or guaranteed reconciliation of fields absent from the source.
